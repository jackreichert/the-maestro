# Cost plugin

Load with [Retro](../SKILL.md) when selected. Goal: lower cost per useful outcome without lowering quality.

- Separate billed totals, estimates, and counterfactual savings. Compare similar workload/complexity; lower spend may mean less delivered. Record source/window and resumed-session attribution/deduplication limits.
- Inspect model mix, fresh/cache-read/cache-write tokens, context growth/compaction/recovery, agent wakes/duplication, scout scope/output size, and review/fix rounds. Use metadata and safe ledger evidence, not raw transcripts or sensitive contents.
- Compare recent baselines; roughly 20% movement against a seven-day median is an investigation flag, not causal proof. Pair efficiency with delivery, rework, review misses, and recovery accuracy. Cheaper-model estimates alone do not prove savings.
- Propose at most two experiments through Retro's lifecycle, with workload-aware metrics, quality floor, observation period, budget, rollback, and revisit date. Monitoring may remain long-running; no daily automatic collection or universal model quotas.
